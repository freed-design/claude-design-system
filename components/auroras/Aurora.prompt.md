Use `Aurora` for a single organic shape on transparency — the smallest aurora unit, for when a float is too much and a full-bleed is far too much.

```jsx
<Aurora shape="pangolin" vibe="soft" blur="high" width={520} />
```

- Shape × `vibe` (soft | intense) × `blur` (low | high). Soft reads as a pale wash; intense carries saturated colour. High blur has no discernible edge; low blur keeps the silhouette readable.
- Exported so far: **willytheworm** (long horizontal S), **pangolin** (crescent), **slinky** (wiggle — intense only). The other seven approved shapes (amoeba, boomerang, bounce, doggy, sloth, stanley, wave) warn and fall back until their PNGs land.
- Purple only for now. Other colorways exist in Figma but haven't been exported.
- Still governed by the one-aurora-per-view rule — a single doesn't share a screen with a float or a full-bleed. Two or more singles combined is a **float**, not a composition you assemble ad hoc.
- Transparent, so it layers over white or photography. Keep the mass off-centre.
